Audio samples in this folder
============================

All .mp3 files in this folder are derived from the Versilian Community Sample
Library (VCSL), https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL , released under
CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Source instruments: Folk Harp, Concert Harp, Bowed and Plucked Psaltery,
Baroque Soprano and Alto Recorder, Frame Drum, Darbuka, Finger Cymbals,
Tambourine, Claps, Small Shaker.

Modifications: single notes/hits selected, silence trimmed, shortened with a
fade-out, high-pass filtered (45 Hz), loudness-normalized, converted to mono
32 kHz MP3 (64 kbps). No attribution is required under CC0; credit is given
here as a courtesy to the library's authors and contributors.
