Privacy Policy

Lex Stack does not collect, share or send any personal data to a server. The game contains no ads, tracking or analytics.

The names and scores in the Hall of Fame are stored only on your device and are never sent anywhere.

The stored information is your best score, the Hall of Fame list, and your sound and language settings. It is kept only in your browser's storage (localStorage) on your device and can be deleted by clearing your browser data.

For questions, you can get in touch through the project page on GitHub.
